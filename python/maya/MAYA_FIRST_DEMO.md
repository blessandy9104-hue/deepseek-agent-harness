# Maya First Proof-of-Value Demo

Position Maya as a local, supervised assistant that turns interests into opportunity briefs, small experiments, and reviewed task plans.

Demo sequence:
1. Start the Maya desktop app.
2. Ask: Maya, what can you help me with?
3. Ask: Map three realistic opportunities using my coding, research, and design interests.
4. Show the opportunity report: problem, offer, skills, startup cost, risks, first experiment, sources/assumptions.
5. Ask Maya to turn the strongest option into a proposed task list.
6. Open the approval dashboard and show that proposals remain review-only.
7. Ask a public research question and show labeled sources.
8. Click Presence STATUS, then Presence OFF.

Acceptance criteria: no income guarantee, no financial action, public/read-only research, approval-gated memory and proposals, visible Presence safeguards, and no upgrade until a bottleneck is measured.

First market experiment: deliver one manually reviewed opportunity brief or research brief to a real person and measure whether it saves time or clarifies a decision.
